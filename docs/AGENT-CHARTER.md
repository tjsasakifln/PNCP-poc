# Agent charter: legacy migration only

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `RUNTIME-AUTHORITY.md` and CONFENGE ADR-STRAT-002 before any
work in this repository.

Allowed work:

- inventory and selectively harvest assets for `web-cfg`, `extra-cli` or Warmbly;
- plan and verify URL-specific redirects approved by web-cfg #62;
- preserve migration evidence and meet security, privacy and retention duties;
- contain incidents and remove obsolete infrastructure;
- complete SmartLic #2111/#2115 and archive the repository.

Forbidden work:

- product features, acquisition, pSEO, billing, SmartLic CTAs or public pages;
- rebuilding SmartLic on Railway, Netcup, Supabase or another provider;
- requesting a Railway token, limit increase or service restoration;
- treating `smartlic.tech` health as a prerequisite for CONFENGE revenue or inbound;
- deploying FastAPI, Next.js, Redis, ARQ or workers as a SmartLic product stack.

If Railway is unavailable, accelerate retirement or migration. Do not restore
the abandoned architecture.
